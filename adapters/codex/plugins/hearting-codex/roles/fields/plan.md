# plan

Plan is the work of settling, in discussion with the user, what is to be built, and writing down what was settled.

## Fundamentals (not dropped)

- What to build is settled by talking it through with the user. Ask first what problem is being solved rather than which solution, and settle together the scope (what will not be done), the conditions to keep, and how we will know it is right. Do not guess what is not known; ask. Count something as settled only when I have reflected back what I understood and the user has said it is right. — Problem, whose need, shared understanding, a test of being right, and scope go together. [1–9, 11]
- What was settled is written down briefly, with the reason it was settled that way. An idea or an assumption that has not been confirmed is marked separately and not mixed in with what was settled. — The reason and the assumptions stay with the record. [1, 2, 3, 5, 7]
- Implementation takes what is written as its standard. When it has to differ from what is written, or meets something not written where the user's intent is at stake, do not guess and fill in; ask the user. If it is decided to change, the written text is corrected first. — What was settled is expected to change, and the change is not made behind anyone's back. [1, 2, 3, 6]

## Not decided here

These differ from person to person and team to team, and this page leaves them open: whether a decision is written down at the moment it is made or later, how detailed the record is, who has the final say (one person or the group together), how long a discovery lasts, which risks to look at first, which techniques are used (interviews, observation, prototypes), whether the conversation leads or a baseline document does, and whether checking the finished build with the user belongs to this field or to another.

## Boundaries with other fields

- Plan is a discussion that the opening session has with the user. The material that supports it (outside research, analysis, a design draft) belongs to `research`, `design`, and the like, and plan takes it in.
- Building from what is written is `code` (or the field that makes the thing).

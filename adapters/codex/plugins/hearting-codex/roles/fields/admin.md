# admin

Admin is the work of producing papers that are submitted in a form someone else has laid down: official letters, project reports, patent responses, applications, handover documents.

## Fundamentals (not dropped)

- Follow the form and the deadline set by the receiving side. — The form, the deadline, and the requirements are set by the receiving side (the law, the agency, the notice), and the person writing cannot change them. [1–6]
- A person with authority signs off. — A document stands only once a person with the authority has approved or certified it. [1–6]
- Leave a trail. — Keep a registration number, a signature and date, a filing, a retention record, and version control. [1–6]
- Write so it is easy to understand. — Use terms and sentences the reader can follow. [1–6]

## Not decided here

These differ from person to person and agency to agency, and this page leaves them open: how far sign-off is delegated (the agency's own rules decide), what may be skipped and how the reason is recorded, which submission system is used, how a handover is done, and how early before the deadline to submit.

## Boundaries with other fields

- Writing the body of a document is `docs`. The form, the deadline, sign-off, submission, and keeping the record are admin.
- A patent response is an argument in writing, but its deadlines and the allowed scope of amendment are fixed by law, so it sits closer to admin.

## Review Focus

Verification rigor, reviewer roles, parallelism, and retry behavior come from
the selected intensity and compiled stage graph. This reference defines the
review questions when a quality or fact-check role is selected; it does not
select reviewers or launch review work.

- **Quality focus:** completeness, logical soundness, venue norms, and coverage
  of rebuttal points.
- **Fact-check focus:** compare claims with the named source materials and
  verify citation, venue, metric, year, and classification. The eight-row
  classification table remains owned by
  [`roles/units/research/fact-check.md`](../../../roles/units/research/fact-check.md).

The selected review node returns its findings through the existing pipeline
handoff. The owner/runtime controls dispatch, retry lineage, and completion.
